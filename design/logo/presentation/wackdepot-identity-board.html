What we heard

The brief

A GitHub-Desktop-class Git client rebuilt in Rust on gpui: frosted glass, lime accent, quick and a little playful. The logo has to live next to Safari, Xcode and Terminal in the Dock, at 16 px in a README badge, and in the app's own titlebar.

nativeplayfulquickcrafted

Success: reads at 16 px, holds in one colour, unmistakably not a branch-with-nodes Git icon, and gives the empty states and mascot a character to grow from.

Commit Buddy

The mascot is the commit: a cheerful blob sitting on the history line.

  • The face is the product's tone of voice — warm where every other Git client is technical
  • The line through it is history, so the mark still says what the app does
  • One shape at every size: Dock icon, 16 px favicon, in-app mascot, empty states
